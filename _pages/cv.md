**Jean-Michel Loubes** — Senior Researcher, Statistician, AI Expert
Research Director at Inria, on leave from Professor of Mathematics (Exceptional Class), University of Toulouse.
Contact: [jean-michel.loubes@inria.fr](mailto:jean-michel.loubes@inria.fr)

Senior researcher and professor of mathematics specializing in machine learning, optimal transport, and the fairness, explainability and robustness of AI. Extensive experience leading interdisciplinary research programs and laboratories, advising European and national regulatory bodies, and building industrial partnerships.

Current positions
======
* **Scientific Director, French AI Safety Institute (INESIA)** and Director of the national AI Evaluation Program
* **Research Director (Directeur de Recherche), Inria** (2024–present)
  * 2025: Creation of the research team *Regulation of AI* (Regalia)
  * 2025–present: Scientific direction of the national research program on risk assessment of AI
  * 2024–2025: Representative of Inria at the CEN-CENELEC committee on AI standards
* **Principal Investigator, Chair TRIAL (Trustworthy AI & Machine Learning), ANITI** — Artificial and Natural Intelligence Toulouse Institute (2019–2023, 2023–2027)

Academic career

* **2024–present:** Research Director, Inria
